[center][size=6][b]Auto Water[/b][/size][/center]

[center][size=3]Let your farm take care of itself.[/size][/center]
[size=3]Tired of watering crops every single day?

With [b]Auto Water[/b], your crops are automatically watered overnight — no sprinklers, no effort, no wasted time. Wake up every morning knowing your farm is ready to grow while you focus on what really matters.

Go mining. Build relationships. Explore. Make money.
Let your crops handle themselves.[/size]
[line]
[size=5][b][u]Features[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Automatically waters supported crops and garden pots at the start of each day[/*]
[*]Includes loaded interiors of farm buildings and other supported locations[/*]
[*]No need for sprinklers or manual watering[/*]
[*]Fully configurable via [b]Generic Mod Config Menu (GMCM)[/b][/*]
[*]Toggle the mod on/off anytime using a configurable hotkey[/*]
[*]Lightweight and performance-friendly[/*]
[/list]
[line]
[size=5][b][u]Requirements and Compatibility[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Stardew Valley 1.6.x[/*]
[*][b]SMAPI 4.2.0 or later[/b][/*]
[*]Generic Mod Config Menu is optional[/*]
[/list]
[line]
[size=5][b][u]How to Install[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Install Stardew Valley 1.6.x and SMAPI 4.2.0 or later[/*]
[*]Download and extract this mod into your "Stardew Valley/Mods" folder[/*]
[*]Run the game using SMAPI[/*]
[/list]
[line]
[size=5][b][u]How to Use[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Install and run the game once to generate the config file[/*]
[*]Configure the mod using Generic Mod Config Menu or by editing the config file[/*]
[*]Use the configured hotkey to toggle the mod on/off at any time[/*]
[*]The setting is saved and applies during gameplay[/*]
[/list]
[line]
[size=5][b][u]Notes[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Only the host performs the watering in multiplayer[/*]
[*]Custom locations and building interiors are processed when exposed through the game's loaded location collections[/*]
[*]Designed to be simple and non-intrusive[/*]
[/list]
