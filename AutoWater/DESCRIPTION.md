[center][size=6][b]Auto Water[/b][/size][/center]

[center][size=3]Let your farm take care of itself.[/size][/center]
[size=3]Tired of watering crops every single day?

With [b]Auto Water[/b], your crops are automatically watered overnight — no sprinklers, no effort, no wasted time. Wake up every morning knowing your farm is ready to grow while you focus on what really matters.

Go mining. Build relationships. Explore. Make money.
Let your crops handle themselves.[/size]

[size=5][b][u]✨ Features[/u][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Automatically waters all crops at the start of each day[/*]
[*]No need for sprinklers or manual watering[/*]
[*]Fully configurable via [b]Generic Mod Config Menu (GMCM)[/b][/*]
[*]Toggle the mod on/off anytime using a configurable hotkey[/*]

[/list]
